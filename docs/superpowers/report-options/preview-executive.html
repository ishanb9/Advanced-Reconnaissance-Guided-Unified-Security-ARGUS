Section 4

Methodology & CVSS Rubric

ARGUS follows a structured Plan → Execute → Validate methodology over a hypothesis tree. Every action is annotated with its MITRE ATT&CK technique and linked to a concrete evidence artefact. A finding is only shipped when it passes the ARGUS Issue-Validator gate — requiring a reproducible, tool-evidenced attack path.

1 · Plan

Surface mapping, hypothesis generation, tool selection. Scope constraints loaded; prior knowledge graph seeded from nmap / banner grabs.

2 · Execute

Ordered tool dispatch against each hypothesis. Every action is logged with timestamp, tool invocation, raw output digest, and MITRE annotation. Non-exploitable paths are recorded as coverage evidence.

3 · Validate

Issue-Validator gate requires: reproducible PoC, concrete tool evidence, accurate CVSS scoring, and retest scheduling. Findings not meeting the bar are demoted to informational.
