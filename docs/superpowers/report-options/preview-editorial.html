03
Methodology

Methodology & CVSS Rubric

How ARGUS plans, executes, and validates. Every action is MITRE-annotated and evidence-chained before a finding ships.

Engagement Process

P
Plan — Hypothesis Tree
ARGUS constructs a structured hypothesis tree of all plausible attack vectors given scope and reconnaissance data. Each hypothesis is prioritised by CVSS pre-exploitation likelihood and banked for ordered execution.
E
Execute — Tool-Driven Exploration
Each hypothesis is tested via purpose-built tool calls. All tool invocations are logged with timestamps, raw output, and evidence hashes.
V
Validate — Issue-Validator Gate
A finding only ships when it passes the ARGUS Issue-Validator: the exploit must be reproducible, the evidence must be concrete, and the CVSS vector must be defensible.
R
Retest — Remediation Verification
After the client remediates, ARGUS retests each finding and updates its status (Open / Verified-Fixed / Re-confirmed).
MITRE Annotation Policy
Every offensive action is tagged with one or more MITRE ATT&CK technique IDs at execution time, enabling direct mapping to defensive controls without post-hoc retro-fitting.
